# Brand Base — Fundacao Visual para Prompts

> Incluir esses elementos em TODOS os prompts de geracao de imagem da marca.

## Paleta

- **Primaria**: Preto + laranja/ambar (#F59E0B, #D97706)
- **Secundaria**: Cinza escuro (#1F2937), branco (#FFFFFF)
- **Aplicacao**: "Black and warm amber/orange color palette, energetic without aggression"

## Estilo fotografico

- Mobile-quality, autentico, natural lighting
- Sem filtro pesado, sem pose artificial
- Bastidor > producao polida
- "Shot on iPhone 16 Pro, natural perspective, slightly imperfect framing"
- "Authentic mobile photography, behind-the-scenes feel"

## Sujeito padrao

- Homem brasileiro atletico, 20-30 anos
- Fisico natural (nao bodybuilder extremo)
- Expressao genuina, relatavel
- "Athletic young Brazilian man in his mid-20s, natural muscular build, genuine relaxed expression"

## Ambientes

- Academia real (nao studio fitness premium)
- Casa com meal prep
- Treino ao ar livre
- "Real gym environment with visible equipment, natural wear and imperfections"

## Anti-patterns (NUNCA)

- Fisico bodybuilder exagerado
- Expressao agressiva "gym bro"
- Iluminacao de studio perfeita
- Estetica stock photo
- Color grading pesado
- Neon/LED gym aesthetic
- Poses artificiais de modelo

## Formatos

| Tipo | Ratio | Resolucao | Uso |
|------|-------|-----------|-----|
| Post IG | 1:1 | 1K | Feed Instagram |
| Story/Reel | 9:16 | 1K | Stories, Reels covers |
| Thumb YouTube | 16:9 | 2K | Thumbnails YouTube |
| Capa Ebook | 2:3 | 2K | Capas de ebook |

## Ancora de prompt (adicionar no final)

"Ultra-realistic, high resolution, authentic mobile photography aesthetic, warm amber and black tones"
